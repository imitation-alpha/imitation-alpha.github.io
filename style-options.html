Plan–Define–Act: a workflow for agentic coding

A simple three-phase loop that keeps multi-step LLM coding tasks tractable, debuggable, and able to recover from being wrong.

Most LLM coding agents fail in one of three predictable ways: they jump in without a plan, they plan in words but never define what done looks like, or they "act" in tiny steps that lose the thread. I've been using a three-phase workflow — see the original notes — that addresses all three.

A good plan is one a colleague could read in 30 seconds and tell you which step is wrong.
